# Research Objective: Empirical Analysis of Crime Disparities
## Investigation of Violent Crime Suspect Rates (BKA PKS 2023, Offense Key 892000) Accounting for Demographic, Socioeconomic, and Registry Confounders

### 1. Research Question
In public discourse, the crude suspect rate (*Tatverdächtigenbelastungszahl*, TVBZ) published in the Police Crime Statistics (*Polizeiliche Kriminalstatistik*, PKS) by the Federal Criminal Police Office (*Bundeskriminalamt*, BKA) is frequently interpreted as direct evidence of an inherently higher delinquency propensity among non-German nationals. In the 2023 reporting year, the crude TVBZ for violent crime (Key 892000) was **152.4** per 100,000 for Germans compared to **584.1** for non-Germans—representing an apparent crude disparity ratio of **3.83×** (and **4.34×** among males aged 14+).

The objective of this project is to deconstruct this crude disparity scientifically, transparently, and auditably according to the standards of the **Responsible Demographics & Research Protocol**. Specifically, the project investigates:
1. To what extent do **denominator artifacts** (non-resident populations such as tourists, transit travelers, cross-border commuters, and persons without permanent residence) mathematically inflate the non-German rate?
2. What proportion of the remaining disparity is attributable to **demographic structural composition** (age and sex differences between the aging German resident population and the younger non-German resident population)?
3. What role do **socioeconomic conditions** (income poverty, cramped housing conditions, labor market restrictions) play as explanatory **mediators** along the causal pathway?
4. What is the magnitude of the residual disparity when comparing structurally homogeneous risk cohorts while accounting for darkfield and reporting biases?

### 2. Scope and Methodological Demarcation
* **Administrative Scope of the PKS**: The PKS is an operational police investigation registry of recorded suspects at the close of preliminary proceedings, not a judicial conviction registry and not an unrecorded delinquency (darkfield) survey.
* **Citizenship vs. Migration Background**: The PKS records exclusively formal legal nationality at the time of the offense. German citizens with a migration history or dual nationals holding a German passport are categorized as Germans.
* **Separation of Registry Accounting and Model Projections**: Pure numerator and denominator counts from official registries (BKA, Destatis) are strictly demarcated from multivariate econometric models and literature-based darkfield transfers.
